One learning page carries what it cost and what it returned, and every field on it points somewhere: to the raw session receipt that proves it, to the initiative Log that earned it, to the index row, the fixed hub, and the log row that make it findable next month. An event that ends only in chat history is an event the business forgets; a banked one lands where recall will find it.

Step 1 · the money fields

Every lesson carries its price tag.

cost takes dollars when they are real, and time always counts ("2 hrs", "3 wks"). result takes what happened, in numbers where numbers exist ("reply rate 4% -> 9% on 50 sends"). status reads high only when the result would likely repeat.

What failed gets written as a failure, in plain words. A flop reported as "mixed results" poisons every future recall that touches it.

The admission gate

Ask before you assert.

Reopen and quote the actual tracker or log row, and recompute calculated values from named inputs. Never present a quantity, duration, sample size, date, or causal motive in prose before asking the founder for it or citing a receipt for it.

Unknown effort or sample size stays blank on purpose: the note shape never justifies guessing.

Dedupe · supersession

One canonical truth, with its history visible.

Search the index, the fixed hub, and the target folder first. Found substantially the same ground: update it, never clone it — fold the new evidence in, adjust the confidence, bump updated.

When today's evidence reverses yesterday's note, the note becomes the current truth and the old claim is kept as superseded, with why. Near-duplicates are how a vault rots into a junk drawer.
