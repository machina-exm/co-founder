Data flow · /co-founder:research

Raw claims in. Only graded receipts out.

Advice built on training data is pattern-matching dressed as knowledge. This skill goes and finds what is true right now, checks it, cites it, and files it where the business can use it again.

The tier sets the sweep

quick 2-3 sources standard 5-8 sources deep opt-in

Quick is a fact to check in minutes. Standard is a decision input from multiple angles. Deep is bet-the-business only, and the founder says yes first because it costs real time and tokens. Uncertain resolves upward.

Three grades, no fourth

  • Verified. Independent sources agree.
  • Stated. One source claims it, so it travels as a quote and never as truth.
  • Contested. Sources disagree, and both sides go into the page.

The deep tier adds an adversarial pass whose only job is to break each load-bearing claim.

Findings can be external facts without pretending to know what this business should do.

Ask before you assert. Never present a quantity, duration, sample size, date, or causal motive in prose before asking the founder for it or citing a receipt for it.

The receipts contract

A benchmark applied to this business, a "should" answer, or any other consequential recommendation needs one internal receipt and one verified external source. One leg missing and it ships as a parked line, never as a call.

PARKED: internal receipt missing PARKED: external receipt missing

/co-founder:research · receipts or it doesn't ship

Capture beats recall

The sweep runs multiple phrasings per question — the practitioner's wording, the vendor's, the skeptic's — because one phrasing finds one bubble. Every candidate claim is captured with its source as it arrives, since an uncited claim does not exist.

Negative findings carry the exact query, engine, date, and count of results inspected.

The admission gate

Before anything is filed, the skill reopens every cited page and confirms the passage supports the written claim, keeping the short quote beside the citation.

A source that vanished, or that turns out not to hold the evidence, is removed: the claim drops to stated with its honest source, or it is dropped.

The filing gate

The audit script runs on every touched durable file, including the complete page. Violations are fixed and it reruns until it exits zero.

Chat gets the answer in 3-6 lines, the strongest receipt, and the link. Next month's question starts from the file, never from a re-run.
