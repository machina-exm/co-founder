The parameter gate decides what exists.

Inventory every parameter the design would need: delivery input, capacity, cap, spot count, guarantee window, buyer behavior, and launch audience. Run each through the shared parameter gate before applying a heuristic.

A missing value gets one question or a parked label, and is excluded from current terms and from the customer-facing close. A heuristic supplies a question, never an invented input.

Both legs, or the element parks.

Every element of the final recommendation carries both legs: at least one fact about their business — a past payment, a margin, a churn number, an audience count — and at least one checked external comparable from research.

A leg missing moves that element to Parked recommendations and names recall or research as the next action. "Needs your call" is only for a judgment fork after the evidence is present.

ruleThe heuristics choose the questions. The receipts justify the answers.

Below the stack, sizing stops big moves.

Public price changes, repricing existing customers, and anything that materially moves revenue are BIG, and go to the gauntlet before any design happens. A small reversible test sizes small only when its bounded audience, cost, and rollback are recorded.

A BIG label has no experiment-shaped bypass. Discovering mid-design that the move needed interrogation is discovering it too late.
