Quadrant · /co-founder:review

Receipts decide what counts. The ration decides what changes.

The weekly scorecard sorts every candidate on two distinctions the skill already enforces: whether the claim is anchored in a quoted receipt or merely founder-stated, and whether the change is internal and reversible or consequential. Position decides which contract a candidate must clear before it can become the week's one change.

The admission gate · step 1

Scores come only from receipts.

  • Reopen every cited file and quote the row that supports the claim.
  • A one-row tracker proves one logged block, not a 14-day measurement.
  • Monthly recurring revenue is a run-rate, not weekly cash or weekly net.
  • A result with no instrument row is Founder-stated: unverified.
  • When a downstream note repeats a claim, check the original receipt, never the note.

Consent surfaces · T1

The exact words, or no field.

  • Capture the exact yes in an immutable raw/inbox/session-receipt-<date>-<slug>.md and link it before the change or any decision row.
  • Lifecycle carries its own anchors, approval_receipt and publication_receipt. An edit to one phrase is not whole-artifact approval.
  • Without the anchor, write no Founder's yes: field; a supplied date never substitutes for the words.

The ration · step 3 and the cadence

1 max

Change to a system, a habit, a cadence or an offer's mechanics, per week, defended out loud: the problem, the scorecard evidence, and what would prove it worked by next review.

  • Zero changes is a legitimate outcome; a machine that worked does not need improving to feel productive.
  • 12 review rows since the last vision row, or 84+ days since vision.md changed, adds a Vision nudge.
  • Nothing is done until scripts/graph-audit exits zero on every touched file.

Moved

What actually advanced, with the receipt that proves it.

Stalled

What was supposed to move and did not, verbatim and unlaundered. Softer wording covering the same week is a lie with better manners.

The money

What came in, what went out, what it says, from the period's dated rows in metrics.md. Not tracked for this period is a legitimate, flagged entry.

The vault

Lessons banked this week, plus notes now past their review_after date, flagged for steward's next health-check.

The score

On track, behind or off, against the vision's quarter goal, in one line with the number that says so.
