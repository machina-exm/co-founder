Drill down before you decide.

Recall walks one path down the vault — the catalog, then the fixed hub, then the page — and every fact it hands back carries the exact file it came from. A look beats a guess, and memory beats re-research on every call worth making.

Read the field, not the prose

Step 3
  • An offer is approved only at offer_state: approved or active.
  • Publication exists only at content_state: published with a published_at value.
  • Contradictory index or hub prose is reported as drift, not repeated as truth.
  • Every type: content page is excluded from business-fact receipts. Drafts are proposals, published pieces are publication history.
  • The current offer comes from wiki/business/offer.md; current numbers come from wiki/business/metrics.md.
  • Notes past their review_after date get flagged as possibly stale.
  • Trace derived claims back to their original inputs. Cross-file analogies and patterns pass the shared parameter gate before they count.
  • Feeding gauntlet, plan, or offer: deliver the 5-10 lines that bear on THIS decision, never a vault dump.

The absence guard

Step 2
  • Absence gets verified, never remembered. Answering from memory instead of searching is the single most common way systems like this fail.
  • Before anything shaped like "we have no note on that": list the relevant wiki/ folders and search the question's key words and their obvious synonyms with whatever the environment has.
  • Genuinely empty: say exactly that, in one line, and offer the research skill to fill it.
  • The scoped result is all that gets phrased. It can prove what is not recorded in the vault, never what the business or the world has never done.

Answer with receipts

Step 3
  • Cite the exact file for every claim — wiki/learnings/warm-intro-beats-cold.md, not "our notes".
  • Reopen the file, verify its current lifecycle and confidence, and quote the exact supporting passage. A page that mentions the topic without the claimed evidence does not pass.
  • When no admitted receipt states it, label it Unverified synthesis: and offer the one founder question that could confirm it.
  • Never present a quantity, duration, sample size, date, or motive before asking for it or citing a receipt for it.

Bank the synthesis

Step 4
  • A comparison, a pattern across notes, a connection nobody had written down — that is itself a finding.
  • Append one pending entry to queue.md from recall to steward, with source_paths naming every note it came from. Recall reads; it never creates the wiki page.
  • Run scripts/graph-audit queue.md, fix every violation, rerun. No queue ID is narrated until it exits zero.
  • Routine lookups are not banked. The test is whether the answer contains something no single existing page says.
