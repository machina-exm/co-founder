Pyramid · /co-founder:vision · the interrogation stack

Five interrogation layers narrow a fuzzy picture into one founder-owned target.

Every layer is a real interview layer, and nothing is chosen for the founder: the quarter target at the apex stays theirs, labelled founder-stated until evidence verifies it.

Step 0 · preflight

Ready, or nothing gets written

A Fresh, Partial, or Wrong-folder result routes to setup — re-sync for Partial — and stops without writing. On Ready, the charter and its path map are read before the vault, and every path comes from that charter.

Step 1 · stub intake

The stub is input, never the vision

A stub may carry business, audience, quarter goal, and founder laws: they map into a candidate direction, candidate sacrifices, and This quarter. Diagnosis and sacrifice are in no stub — the interview elicits them.

Step 3 · the checkpoint

One screen, judgeable without homework

The diagnosis in 2 lines, the direction in 2-3, the not-list, the quarter goal in 1 line, and anything that needs their call. Corrections integrate and the whole screen re-presents: a revision is never a confirmation.

The receipts gate

Cleared, or parked and said so

A direction is cleared only when the internal receipt points outside initiative and content output, the external receipt points to a dated research file, and graph-audit verifies both. Otherwise the state is parked and the direction is not presented as ratified strategy.

Mandatory filing gate

It reruns until it exits zero

graph-audit runs on every touched durable file. Every violation is fixed and it runs again: the vision write stays incomplete until it exits zero.

Revisions

The business remembers what it used to believe

A revision runs the same interview on what changed. A direction change that is hard to reverse, surprising, and a real tradeoff earns a decision row with its why, and the old text survives inside the file under Superseded <date>.

The stub close

Four changes, one close

On the first canonical write the stub link is replaced in the catalog and the business hub, the stub file is deleted, and any pending pointer naming it is reconciled — one linked-state transaction, no dangling entry.

Step 5 · hand off

Two lines, one next move

The direction in one sentence, then the single next move: an active initiative gets re-checked against the new vision by review, and a fresh start goes to plan — "what's the plan for the quarter goal".
