/co-founder · content-engine · quality tiers

Content refines upward, one gate at a time.

This engine runs on three inputs before any writing happens: the voice file, the vault, and distilled craft mechanics. Nothing climbs a tier on plausibility — each promotion is a named gate with a receipt behind it.

type: content channel: writing · X · newsletter state: draft → approved → published

Loaded before a word is written

step 0
  • Voice. Register, words and moves, the never-list, the approved samples. Missing: stop and route to setup.
  • Facts. Content is generated from these canonical vault facts, never from generic knowledge about the founder's industry.
  • Scope. The v1 craft block covers writing, X, and newsletter; another channel routes to setup re-sync and stops.

What a promotion refuses

admission
  • Reopen the source, quote the exact supporting line, and verify subject plus unit: a client count cannot become a personal-post count or proof of a client outcome.
  • Never assert a duration, a count, a history, or a habit about the founder unless those exact words appear verbatim in a raw/ file or the live transcript.
  • No receipt: the claim comes out or gets researched. Inventing a statistic ships under the founder's name.
  • A public call-to-action missing a receipt leg is recorded under Parked recommendations, never dropped silently.

The filing gate

step 4
  • Run scripts/graph-audit on the complete content artifact and every other touched durable file, then fix every violation and rerun until it exits zero.
  • A founder-confirmed publication cannot remain draft; a draft cannot be counted as shipped.

Publishing and scheduling stay with the founder: this environment writes drafts, the founder ships them. A draft is never a canonical business fact.
