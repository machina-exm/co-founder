Every state that cannot enter planning

The gate check reads the stages in order, stopping at the first one missing. Interviewed, never scolded: name it, ask the first missing question.

Vision missing

The founder is planning against nothing. Route to vision and stop: no initiative created or sized, no queue entry changed, no log row.

"Just build it", "go"

Refuse the build, start the scope. Create at most an idea stub; the missing stage is planned.

idea · BIG bet

"This is weeks of your life, it gets interrogated before it gets planned." The gauntlet alone flips survived.

survived · DIES or PARKED

Routes back to the named evidence gap without a state change. Only SURVIVES with no Parked recommendation proceeds.

planned

Replanning, not a new transition. Preserve the state, checkpoint the changed scope, update the roadmap without erasing history.

executing

A changed destination creates a separate idea linking this one; unchecked steps move to Roadmap → Stopped with the reason.

reviewed · banked

The arc is closed. New work gets a new linked idea; the closed file stays unchanged.

unknown · malformed

Stop for repair. Never infer the nearest legal state.

Receipts · state invariant

Two legs, or the initiative stays parked

Every initiative advancing beyond idea carries both checked references in frontmatter. Missing fields keep it PARKED and below planned, independent of wording or size.

  • internal_receipt — business evidence outside initiative and content output.
  • external_receipt — dated checked research in wiki/research/.
  • A missing leg moves the item to Parked recommendations, never to Needs your call.

Step 3 · scope the WHAT

The checkpoint, on one screen

Written before the Scope and Roadmap sections or any state change. Every line must be judgeable without homework.

  • What we're doing — 2-3 lines in their words.
  • Key tradeoffs — the calls made and why.
  • Out of scope — what this deliberately is not.
  • Needs your call — only they can rule on it.
a revision is never a confirmation

The flip is one linked transaction
